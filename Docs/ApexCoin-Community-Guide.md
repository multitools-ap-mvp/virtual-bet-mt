## Quick start

1. **Claim your daily reward.** Tap the coin on the Matches page to get **300 ApexCoin**. You can claim again 24 hours after your last claim.

2. **Pick a match.** Open matches show the teams and how much of the pool is on each one.

3. **Place a bet.** Tap a team, enter an amount, check the estimated payout and confirm.

4. **Wait for the result.** An admin confirms the winner and payouts land in your balance.

5. **Check your Currency tab** (tap your balance at the top right) to see your ApexCoin, level and every transaction.

## How betting works

**When you can bet.** Before a match starts. Betting closes when the match begins.

**How much.** Any whole amount, from 1 ApexCoin up to your balance.

**Pooled betting.** Everyone's bets on a match go into one shared pool. When the match ends, everyone who picked the winner **splits the whole pool in proportion to what they bet**. Bet more on the winner and you get a bigger share.

**Results.** An admin confirms each result by hand, and payouts are credited after that.

**Special cases**

| Situation                | What happens                                          |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Match is voided          | Every bet is refunded                                 |
| Outcomes tie             | The pool is split between the tied outcomes           |
| Nobody picked the winner | The whole pot is carried to next avaliable active bet |

## Understanding the odds

There are no fixed odds. The **pool decides the payout**, and it changes as more people bet. Each team shows its **share of the pool**: the smaller the share, the bigger the payout if that team wins.

> **Example.** A match has three outcomes, with 10,000 ApexCoin in the pool:


| Team | In the pool | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Team1 | 5,200 | 52% |
| Team2 | 3,100 | 31% |
| Team3 | 1,700 | 17% |

If you bet **100 ApexCoin**, the estimated payout *if that team wins* is:

| Your pick | Estimated payout | Roughly |
|---|---|---|
| Team1 (favorite) | about 191 | 1.9× |
| Team2 | about 316 | 3.2× |
| Team3 (long shot) | about 561 | 5.6× |

**Good to know**

- The estimate on the bet sheet uses the pool **right now**. If more bets come in, the pool and your payout change.
- Your final payout is based on the pool when betting closes.
- Your own bet counts in the pool too, so a very large bet on a small outcome lowers its own payout.

## Levels and bonuses

Your **level** goes up when your ApexCoin balance reaches the next amount. Each level pays a **one-time bonus**, and the bonus **doubles at every level**.

| Level | Badge | Reach this balance | Bonus |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Rookie | 🟤 Bronze | Start here | None |
| 2. Contender | ⚪ Silver | 500 | **+1,000** |
| 3. Challenger | 🟡 Gold | 2,500 | **+2,000** |
| 4. Champion | 🔷 Platinum | 10,000 | **+4,000** |
| 5. Apex Legend | 🔴 Red | 25,000 | **+8,000** |

- Each bonus is paid **once**, the first time you reach that level.
- Spending or losing ApexCoin **never lowers your level**, and it never pays a bonus twice.
- Bonuses add up to **15,000** ApexCoin if you reach every level.
- Your badge shows on your profile and next to your name on the Ranks page.
